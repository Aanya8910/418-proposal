Cutting the Verify: In-Pass Draft Truncation for Mixture-of-Experts Speculative Decoding

Anya Shankar (anyashan) · Aanya Kochhar (aanyak) · 15-418, Fall 2026

Summary

We will make speculative-decoding verification cheaper for Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) models by deciding during the forward pass, layer by layer, which trailing draft rows are worth the extra expert-weight traffic they cause. We will implement this as a fused, lock-free MoE kernel in Metal on a 32-core M5 Max GPU, comparing three schemes for synchronizing threadgroups across phases, and run memory-consistency litmus tests on the same GPU.
